RiffFinder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

RiffFinder ("the app") is a guitar companion app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing automatically; an error report reaches us only if you decide to send it by email.

Data we collect

Nothing automatically. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no permission to use the internet. We do not store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Error reports (optional)

To help fix problems, the app keeps a technical log on your device: what it did (pages opened, settings changed, files imported or exported with their format and size, sound and microphone start-up), app errors, and, on Android 11 and later, the reason Android gives when the app stopped (crash, not responding, low memory). The log is size-limited, stays in the app's private storage and is erased when you uninstall the app or press Clear in Settings › Error report.

The log is sent only if you choose to: Send by email opens your own email app with the report filled in, addressed to the developer. You can read or edit it, and nothing is sent until you press Send. A report contains the app version, your phone model and Android version, the app settings (language, tuning, capo, theme…), error messages and the recent log. It contains no songs, no audio, no file names, no contacts and no location. Like any email, it shows your email address to the developer. Reports are used only to fix the app, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted once the problem is solved. You can ask for a report to be deleted at the address below.

Microphone

The tuner uses the microphone to detect the pitch of the string you play. Audio is analysed in real time on your device, only while the Tuner page is shown. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can refuse or revoke the permission in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Files

Songs you create are stored locally in the app's storage on your device. When you import or export a tab, the app reads or writes only the file you choose with Android's file picker.

Network

The app does not need an internet connection, has no permission to use it, and does not send data over the network. Error reports go through your email app, only when you send them.

Children

The app collects no data automatically from anyone, including children.
