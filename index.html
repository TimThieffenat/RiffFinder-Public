Privacy Policy

RiffFinder

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the RiffFinder app for Android handles your information. RiffFinder is developed and published by Tim Thieffenat.

In short: RiffFinder works entirely offline. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

1. Data controller

Tim Thieffenat
Email: tim.thieffenat@gmail.com

2. Information the app does not collect

RiffFinder does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Account or login information (the app has no user accounts)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files or other content from your device
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage statistics, analytics or crash reports

The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics tools, and it does not require an internet connection to work.

3. Microphone access (tuner)

The built-in tuner needs access to your device's microphone in order to detect the pitch of your guitar strings. The app only asks for this permission when you use the tuner.

  • Audio is analyzed in real time, directly on your device.
  • Audio is never recorded, saved or stored.
  • Audio is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

You can refuse or revoke microphone access at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Apps > RiffFinder > Permissions). All other features of the app will keep working; only the tuner requires the microphone.

4. Data stored on your device

The app may save your settings and preferences (for example metronome tempo or display options) locally on your device so they are kept between sessions. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your Android settings.

5. Purchases

RiffFinder is a paid app sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google. The developer does not receive or have access to your payment details. Information related to your purchase is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing of data

Since RiffFinder does not collect any personal data, no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.

7. Children's privacy

RiffFinder is not specifically directed at children. Because the app does not collect any personal data, it does not collect personal data from children either.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and object to the processing of your personal data. As RiffFinder does not collect or store any personal data, the developer holds no data about you. For any question, you can contact me at the email address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

9. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk of being exposed from the developer's side. Any settings stored by the app remain within the app's private storage on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated, for example if new features are added to the app. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
